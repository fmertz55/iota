# ICP-Aligned Physics: Further-Research Assessment

Date reviewed: `2026-07-15`

Status: **literature-grounded research agenda; not evidence from the present experiments**

## Recommendation

Add a short, tightly bounded further-research subsection to the eventual
quantum-Dirac paper, but limit it to three technically adjacent programs:

1. canonical fermionic and interacting causal QCA;
2. process-tensor tests of causally retained quantum history; and
3. curved-spacetime Dirac role recovery under locally varying controls.

Constructor theory is worth one positioning paragraph because its
possible/impossible-task language is unusually close to ICP's reachable-future
language. Quantum Darwinism and holographic/tensor-network geometry should
remain in this separate program memo unless later work creates a direct bridge.
They would broaden the paper faster than the current evidence can support.

Nothing in this assessment changes the active direct-QCA protocol, authorizes a
new outcome, or counts as evidence that ICP explains these areas.

## Selection rule

A topic qualifies as ICP-aligned here only if all four items can be stated:

- a precise point of contact with causally retained state, relational topology,
  admissible interventions, or reachable futures;
- the additional physical structure that must be supplied;
- a mathematical or experimental bridge that could fail; and
- the nearest established literature, with novelty left to the proposed bridge
  rather than to the neighboring theory.

Broad slogans such as “information is physical,” “it from bit,” or “spacetime
emerges from entanglement” are not sufficient by themselves.

## Priority 1 — Canonical fermionic and interacting causal QCA

### Why it aligns

The current direct-QCA experiment already exposes the key boundary: its
full-space hard-core qubit gates and a canonical fermionic lift agree in the
one-excitation sector but differ on double occupation. ICP asks whether a
declared informational architecture recovers a target causal role; moving from
a one-particle role to a fermionic field role is therefore a natural breadth
test, not a metaphysical extrapolation.

Farrelly and Short show that strictly causal fermionic evolution in discrete
spacetime admits local decompositions and can be represented within causal
qubit dynamics; they discuss Dirac fields and the interacting Thirring model as
continuum targets: <https://arxiv.org/abs/1303.4652>.

### Supplied structure

- canonical anticommutation or an explicitly equivalent qubit encoding;
- a fermionic vacuum and occupation convention;
- multiparticle encoding, measurement, and intervention maps;
- coupling/renormalization prescriptions for an interacting target; and
- particle-number, scattering, and field-observable gates.

These are substantial new inputs. The present hard-core lift cannot be silently
renamed a fermionic field theory.

### Falsifiable bridge

Create a new search ID and first test the free two-particle antisymmetric sector.
Require uniform convergence of propagators and exchange-sensitive observables,
then add a separately frozen Thirring-type scattering tier. The candidate fails
if the local lift cannot reproduce the target exchange phase, causal cone,
scattering channel, or refinement behavior under fixed maps.

### Paper decision

Include. It is the most direct physical extension of the collision-sector
limitation and tells readers exactly what would be required to move from
single-particle quantum mechanics toward field theory.

## Priority 2 — Process tensors and quantum causal memory

### Why it aligns

ICP defines present information through whatever part of interaction history is
still retained in current state/topology and can change future intervention
responses. Quantum causal modelling distinguishes causal mechanisms through
interventions rather than correlations alone. Costa and Shrapnel formulate
quantum causal models with autonomous mechanisms and interventions:
<https://arxiv.org/abs/1512.07106>.

The process-tensor framework makes multi-time controls explicit. Pollock and
colleagues give an operational Markov condition based on causal breaks and show
that different past controls producing different later conditional states
witness quantum memory: <https://arxiv.org/abs/1801.09811>.

### Supplied structure

- an open-system/environment partition;
- a frozen set of admissible quantum instruments at multiple times;
- an operational state or reduced-state map;
- a causal-break protocol; and
- a process-tensor reconstruction or bounded witness family.

The current closed, fully specified QCA is Markovian at the complete-state
level. Memory becomes a nontrivial question only after an environment or
coarse-graining is declared.

### Falsifiable bridge

Construct pairs of interaction histories that agree under a proposed current
informational state map. Apply a causal break and a frozen basis of later
controls. If later conditional states differ, the proposed current ICP state is
incomplete: some causally retained history was omitted. Conversely, a complete
linearly independent control test can bound the memory dimension required by
the coarse-grained description.

This is a particularly sharp bridge because it can falsify a state definition,
not merely find a qualitative analogy.

### Paper decision

Include. Present it as a formal route for testing the “causally retained
history” clause beyond closed unitary constructions.

## Priority 3 — Curved-spacetime Dirac roles and relational geometry

### Why it aligns

The current experiments supply a fixed periodic geometry and test whether local
information rules recover a flat-spacetime Dirac role. A next-stage question is
whether spatially varying local controls can recover the role associated with a
declared curved metric, and eventually whether a dynamic relational topology
can carry that geometry as causal state.

Arrighi, Facchini, and Forets show that a class of quantum walks has continuum
limits encompassing the massive Dirac equation in `1+1D` curved spacetime and
emphasize encoding and grouping as ingredients of the limit:
<https://arxiv.org/abs/1505.07023>.

### Supplied structure

- a target metric or tetrad field;
- the spin connection and curved Dirac Hamiltonian;
- a frozen map from geometric fields to local gates;
- coordinate-independent observable and intervention maps; and
- curvature, bandwidth, and causal-cone domains.

Supplying a metric and reproducing its test-particle behavior is not deriving
gravity. Allowing topology to change would be a later and more demanding tier.

### Falsifiable bridge

First hold the lattice fixed and encode a bounded family of smooth metrics in
local gate parameters. Test propagation, redshift/phase, current conservation,
and local light-cone behavior under refinement. Then compare two architectures
under the same metric-to-gate map. A subsequent dynamic-topology tier must show
that geometry-relevant history is encoded in the declared topology/state and
must survive interventions that erase or redirect that history.

### Paper decision

Include, with the fixed-background qualification. It is the clearest route from
the flat target toward a relational-geometry question without claiming emergent
gravity.

## Priority 4 — Constructor theory of information

### Why it aligns

Constructor theory expresses information in terms of physical transformations
that are possible or impossible. Deutsch and Marletto explicitly formulate
information through such task possibilities rather than treating it as a
free-standing mathematical primitive: <https://arxiv.org/abs/1405.5563>.

ICP's `R_t(a)` is likewise organized around futures reachable under declared
interventions. The overlap is structural: both approaches treat information
through physically constrained transformations. The difference is equally
important. Constructor theory proposes a general law-level language of tasks;
the current ICP program searches for minimal constructions that recover frozen
causal roles and tracks which interaction history remains active in a present
realization.

### Supplied structure

- a task algebra for preparations, transformations, and measurements;
- a repeatability/constructor convention;
- a translation from `E`, `M`, and `Phi` into task statements; and
- a criterion for approximate rather than exact task possibility.

### Falsifiable bridge

For a finite ICP construction, translate each frozen role and intervention into
constructor-theoretic tasks. Require the translation to preserve task
composition, interoperability, and declared impossibilities. A counterexample
in which two constructions have identical ICP role recovery but different task
possibility structure—or vice versa—would delimit the proposed correspondence.

### Paper decision

Mention briefly in conceptual positioning or future work. Do not imply that ICP
is a derivation of constructor theory or that either framework validates the
other.

## Priority 5 — Quantum Darwinism and spectrum broadcast structure

### Why it aligns

Quantum Darwinism studies the proliferation of multiple environmental records
of selected system states. Zurek frames classical robustness through redundant
records in environmental fragments: <https://arxiv.org/abs/0903.5082>.
Strong Quantum Darwinism and spectrum-broadcast work sharpen the distinction
between mutual information, accessible classical information, and objective
records: <https://arxiv.org/abs/1803.08936>.

This resembles ICP's interest in interaction history that remains causally
available, but the concepts are not equivalent. A retained constraint need not
be redundantly broadcast, and a redundant record is a particular open-system
state structure rather than a general definition of information.

### Supplied structure

- a system/environment split and multiple disjoint fragments;
- pointer observables or a frozen optimization rule;
- accessible-information and discord calculations;
- non-disturbance criteria; and
- interventions that delete, scramble, or isolate record fragments.

### Falsifiable bridge

Test whether an interaction record that satisfies a frozen strong-Darwinism or
spectrum-broadcast gate also remains necessary for a future intervention
response. Then test the converse: whether an ICP-retained causal distinction
can exist without redundant records. Finding either one-way separation would
clarify that “retained,” “accessible,” and “objective” information are distinct
roles.

### Paper decision

Do not develop in the current quantum-Dirac paper. At most include one sentence
pointing to open-system record formation as a separate future tier. It deserves
its own experiment and literature review.

## Exploratory only — Tensor networks, holography, and quantum error correction

Tensor-network work organizes quantum-state information by scale and associates
that structure with emergent geometry; Swingle's entanglement-renormalization
proposal is a primary example: <https://arxiv.org/abs/0905.1317>. Holographic
quantum-error-correcting codes make bulk-to-boundary encoding and reconstruction
explicit in solvable tensor-network models: <https://arxiv.org/abs/1503.06237>.

Possible ICP contact points are relational encoding, reconstruction after local
erasure, and persistence of a logical role across different physical supports.
The gap is large: these frameworks assume substantial Hilbert-space,
entanglement, code, and often holographic structure. The present Dirac result
does not show emergent geometry or error correction.

A credible bridge would freeze a logical bulk role, encoding map, erasure
interventions, reconstruction regions, and resource accounting, then compare
minimal local informational constructions. Until such a protocol exists, keep
this out of the paper's further-research subsection and out of socialization
claims.

## Proposed paper text

The eventual technical paper could use a paragraph of approximately this
scope, revised to match the direct experiment's final disposition:

> The present result is restricted to a supplied flat periodic geometry and a
> single-excitation sector. Three prospective extensions are especially
> concrete. First, replacing the declared hard-core collision lift with a
> canonical fermionic encoding would permit a new, separately frozen test of
> multiparticle and interacting causal-QCA roles. Second, process-tensor causal
> breaks offer an operational test of whether a proposed reduced informational
> state retains every aspect of history that can affect later quantum
> interventions. Third, locally varying walk parameters can target a bounded
> curved-spacetime Dirac role before any stronger claim about dynamically
> emergent geometry. These are proposed experiments, not consequences of the
> current single-particle construction.

Constructor theory can be cited in a separate sentence as a neighboring
possible/impossible-transformation framework. Quantum Darwinism and
holographic/tensor-network geometry should remain outside this paragraph unless
a dedicated bridge has been preregistered.

## Topics explicitly not promoted from analogy

Do not add the following to the current paper without a separate formal audit:

- consciousness or integrated-information claims;
- entropic or emergent gravity;
- Wheeler-style slogans as evidence;
- thermodynamic irreversibility from the present reversible microscopic law;
- particle ontology from the single-excitation encoding;
- gauge fields, curved geometry, or interactions as if already tested; or
- whole-universe computational or cellular-automaton claims.

The governing rule is simple: a nearby theory belongs in the paper only when
the supplied assumptions, frozen map, measurable bridge, and possible failure
can all be stated in advance.
