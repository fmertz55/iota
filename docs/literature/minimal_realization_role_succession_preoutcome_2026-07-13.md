## Closest primary comparisons

### Complete centered binary rule space

Wolfram, *Statistical Mechanics of Cellular Automata*
([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1103/RevModPhys.55.601)), studies the ordinary
one-dimensional binary nearest-neighbor setting that gives eight local
neighborhoods and 256 deterministic rules. The proposed grammar is this
established family in explicit production-row accounting. Neither the family,
its exhaustive enumeration, nor simple rules producing complex patterns is
novel.

Naumov and Shalyto, *Classification of Structures Generated by
One-Dimensional Binary Cellular Automata from a Point Embryo*
([primary paper](https://is.ifmo.ru/articles_en/_classif_cell.pdf)), classify
structures generated by all 256 elementary rules from a point seed. This is a
direct prior-art threat to any claim based on the rule panel plus initial
condition. The present test can at most distinguish its frozen attribution,
one-tick succession, and matched outward-intervention conjunction.

Cook, *Universality in Elementary Cellular Automata*
([DOI](https://doi.org/10.25088/ComplexSystems.15.1.1)), establishes rich
glider-based behavior for Rule 110 on a periodic background. It also warns
against treating a single-event, all-zero-background null as a general
complexity or universality result.

### Localized structures and role persistence

Hanson and Crutchfield, *Computational Mechanics of Cellular Automata: An
Example* ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0167-2789(96)00259-X)), and Rupe and
Crutchfield, *Local Causal States and Discrete Coherent Structures*
([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.5021130)), identify particles or coherent
structures relative to spacetime domains and symmetries. An isolated dyad is a
much narrower observer-fixed role. A null for it does not exclude
domain-relative coherent structure.

Eppstein, *Searching for Spaceships*
([primary preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/cs/0004003)), uses exact constrained
search for translating localized CA patterns under declared width, period, and
speed bounds. This defeats novelty for exact localized-pattern search and
supports explicit resource and topology boundaries.

Beer, *The Cognitive Domain of a Glider in the Game of Life*
([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1162/ARTL_a_00125)), divides perturbations of a glider
into destructive and nondestructive classes. It is the closest established
comparison for testing persistence of an organization under perturbation. The
project's single equal-cost sham/breaking contrast is substantially narrower
and cannot support cognition, autopoiesis, or general robustness.

### Finite orbits and topology dynamics

Martin, Odlyzko, and Wolfram, *Algebraic Properties of Cellular Automata*
([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01223745)), and Tadaki and Matsufuji,
*Periodicity in One-Dimensional Finite Linear Cellular Automata*
([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1143/ptp/89.2.325)), establish finite-ring orbit and
size-dependent period analysis. Exact recurrence on `C64` is a standard finite
state argument, not evidence about an infinite line or another ring size.

Ilachinski and Halpern, *Structurally Dynamic Cellular Automata*
([publisher](https://www.complex-systems.com/abstracts/v01_i03_a07/)), studies
binary site values coupled to locally changing topology. Arrighi and Dowek,
*Causal Graph Dynamics* ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ic.2012.10.019)),
formalize local homogeneous evolution on time-varying graphs. These works
defeat novelty for the unselected explicit-relation-evolution axis and show
why a later dynamic-bond grammar would have to charge its carried link state
and update laws explicitly.

## Effect on the frozen design

The search fixed the following cautions before custom outcomes:

1. Call the grammar an elementary cellular automaton and enumerate all 256
   rules; do not present it as a new rule family.
2. Preserve the existing dyad, successor direction, sham, breaking arm, and
   endpoint so the tested change is the additional incidence read.
3. Publish fan-in, relation-read count, truth-table capacity, storage, work,
   orientation, and seed cost alongside `N_P` and `N_L`.
4. Use `H_pre=29`, derived from the two-layer endpoint and `C64` cone, to
   separate localized pre-wrap evidence from ring-exposed behavior.
5. Trace the full seeded/comparator state for recurrence because row `000` can
   evolve the comparator.
6. Run frozen larger-ring controls before serializing any primary passer. Treat
   pre-wrap cross-size agreement as an algebraic validation, not replication.
7. Keep diagnostic stages separate. A dyad successor can move the observed
   bottleneck; it cannot replace the outward causal endpoint.
8. Use supplied synthetic roles to validate the detector without inspecting a
   canonical candidate trajectory.
9. Make no global-minimality, coherent-structure, universality, cognition,
   autopoiesis, current-physics, or novelty claim.

## Novelty conclusion

No novelty is established for the centered binary grammar, exhaustive rule
enumeration, point-seed search, local persistent patterns, exact finite-orbit
analysis, or perturbation of CA objects. This bounded search did not identify a
primary source using the exact conjunction of the project's normalized
primitive/law ledger, endogenous attributed dyad succession, equal-cost
internal-role intervention, outward positional endpoint, complete pre-wrap
classification, and sealed outcome chronology. Absence of an exact match is
not evidence of novelty. Any useful contribution remains the transparent
conjunction and its negative or positive construction record.
