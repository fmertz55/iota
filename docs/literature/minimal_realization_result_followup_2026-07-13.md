## Closest primary work

### Exhaustive two-input rule families

Alonso-Sanz and Bull, *Elementary Cellular Automata with Minimal Memory and
Random Number Generation* ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.25088/ComplexSystems.18.2.195),
[primary PDF](https://content.wolfram.com/sites/13/2019/03/18-2-2.pdf)), encode
the four input pairs `11`, `10`, `01`, and `00` as rules `0..15`, use
single-site seeds, and use periodic rings. Their two inputs are temporal memory
features rather than this contract's directed spatial pair, so the dynamics are
not equivalent. The work nevertheless removes any broad novelty claim for
enumerating all 16 two-input Boolean maps from a point seed.

Dekking, van Driel, and Fey, *Stability in Random Boolean Cellular Automata on
the Integer Lattice* ([primary manuscript](https://arxiv.org/abs/0704.2183)),
study all 16 maps from two binary inputs and circular-lattice stabilization in
an inhomogeneous Boolean-CA setting. Their neighborhood and per-cell rule
assignment differ from the uniform directed-predecessor grammar. A recurring
`0110` block in that different system also means that the motif alone cannot be
treated as a novel object definition.

Naumov and Shalyto, *Classification of Structures Generated by One-Dimensional
Binary Cellular Automata from a Point Embryo*
([primary PDF](https://is.ifmo.ru/works/classif/Article.pdf)), classify
single-point-seed structures across all 256 elementary cellular automata. Their
rules have three inputs, not two. The comparison rules out novelty for the
general idea of exhaustive single-seed rule classification.

### Localized objects and search bounds

Eppstein, *Searching for Spaceships*
([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/cs/0004003),
[primary MSRI PDF](https://library.slmath.org/books/Book42/files/eppstein.pdf)),
uses constrained state-space search for moving localized CA patterns. The
search is explicitly parameterized by properties such as width, period, and
speed. This is the appropriate precedent for interpreting the present outcome
as a null within declared bounds, not absence of every localized organization.

Hanson and Crutchfield, *Computational Mechanics of Cellular Automata: An
Example* ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0167-2789(96)00259-X)), identify Rule
54 domains, particles, and interactions using domain filters. Hordijk, Shalizi,
and Crutchfield, *Upper Bound on the Products of Particle Interactions in
Cellular Automata* ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0167-2789(01)00252-4),
[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/nlin/0008038)), formalize persistent localized
propagating structures relative to domain and particle periods. These works
show that accepted CA objects need not be isolated motifs on a quiescent
background; they may be defects relative to a structured domain.

Rupe and Crutchfield, *Local Causal States and Discrete Coherent Structures*
([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.5021130),
[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/1801.00515)), define coherent structures as
localized deviations from spacetime-domain symmetries, with temporal
persistence and causal propagation. This is a broader detector family than the
fixed isolated-dyad predicate. The present negative result says nothing about
whether one of the 15 rules has a domain-relative coherent structure under a
different initial condition.

Beer, *The Cognitive Domain of a Glider in the Game of Life*
([publisher](https://direct.mit.edu/artl/article/20/2/183/2768/The-Cognitive-Domain-of-a-Glider-in-the-Game-of),
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1162/ARTL_a_00125)), exhaustively partitions immediate
environment perturbations into identity-preserving and destructive classes for
each glider form. The current single matched sham/break contrast is much
narrower. It supports only the frozen positional-effect endpoint and cannot
support a general robustness, cognition, or autopoiesis claim.

### Horizon, background, and asymptotic caution

Cook, *Universality in Elementary Cellular Automata*
([journal](https://www.complex-systems.com/abstracts/v15_i01_a01/),
[primary PDF](https://wpmedia.wolfram.com/uploads/sites/13/2018/02/15-1-1.pdf)),
uses Rule 110 gliders on periodic ether, with repeating patterns outside the
encoded finite region. Margenstern, *About Strongly Universal Cellular
Automata* ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/1304.6316)), explicitly distinguishes
weak universality on periodic backgrounds from strong universality starting
with finitely many nonquiescent cells. A blank-background, one-seed, 16-layer
null therefore cannot diagnose universality or nonuniversality.

Kari, *The Nilpotency Problem of One-Dimensional Cellular Automata*
([publisher and DOI](https://epubs.siam.org/doi/10.1137/0221036)), proves that
asymptotic nilpotency is undecidable for general one-dimensional cellular
automata. This is context, not a theorem about the present fixed finite system.
The 15 deterministic maps on `C64` have finite joint seeded/baseline state
spaces and can, in principle, be followed until recurrence. Here `H=16` simply
did not perform that exact-orbit closure.

Pita, *How Local Rules Generate Emergent Structure in Cellular Automata*
([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.00273)), derives a forward causal-coupling
architecture from elementary-CA lookup tables. Although it is a preprint and
uses a different three-input grammar, it reinforces the value of analyzing
rule-table causal architecture before choosing a larger dynamical search.

## Effect on the result and next experiment

No novelty is established for any of the following:

- enumerating all two-input Boolean rule tables;
- searching rules from a single active seed on a periodic lattice;
- finite searches for localized CA patterns;
- persistent particles or domain-relative coherent structures;
- perturbational tests of a CA object's identity; or
- causal structure derived from Boolean lookup tables.

The exact combination of the directed `C64` grammar, accounting convention,
single seed, isolated-dyad succession test, outward matched intervention,
`H=16`, and development allocation was not found in this search. Absence from
one web index does not establish novelty. At most, the repository contributes a
transparent grammar-relative negative construction record and a specific
matched-intervention accounting method.

The null is scientifically informative because it localizes the observed
bottleneck before the intervention stage. It does not justify post-outcome
retuning of the object predicate. The strongest next test is a separately
frozen exact-orbit continuation at canonical offset `0`, with grammar, ring,
seed, baseline, and detector unchanged. For each candidate, evolve the joint
seeded/baseline state until either:

1. the full detector passes, producing an exact later `T_proto`; or
2. the complete joint state repeats after the forward cone has saturated the
   cycle, proving no later pass on that fixed finite orbit.

That test is stronger than selecting another arbitrary horizon. It requires no
reserved translations because exact translation equivariance has already been
verified. Even a recurrence-based no-pass would exclude only these 15 rules,
this seed and background, this `C64` topology, and this detector. It would not
exclude alternative `N_P=2` grammars or establish global minimality.
