# PREDICT line: what is prior art, what could be ours (2026-11-10)

Companion to `shadow_prior_art_addendum_embedded_particles_2026-11-09.md`. Question from the user: does PREDICT-001
"roll over prior art with our own terms"? Answer: **the concept does; specific results might not.**

## The concept is established (records checked 2026-11-10)
| Our term | Established name | Source |
|---|---|---|
| histories with the same response to every probe are "the same object state" | Myhill-Nerode equivalence; minimal machine | Nerode, Proc. AMS 1958 (existence confirmed via McNaughton's review, doi 10.2307/2271612) |
| the minimal summary of the past that predicts the future | causal state, epsilon-machine | Shalizi & Crutchfield, J. Stat. Phys. 2001, doi 10.1023/a:1010388907793 |
| minimal state that predicts the response to a probe (input) | epsilon-transducer | Barnett & Crutchfield, J. Stat. Phys. 2015, doi 10.1007/s10955-015-1327-5 |
| state as predictions of probe outcomes | predictive state representation (tests) | Singh, James & Rudary, "Predictive state representations: a new theory for modeling dynamical systems" (arXiv 1207.4167, posted 2012; original venue and year not checked); the earlier Littman, Sutton & Singh paper is cited from memory, record not checked |
| coherent structures in CA space-time as causal-state objects | local causal states, coherent-structure filters | Shalizi et al., PRE 2006, doi 10.1103/physreve.73.036104; Rupe & Crutchfield 2018 (cited) |
| particle catalog + collision table predicts CA behaviour | embedded-particle models | Hordijk, Crutchfield & Mitchell 1996, 1998 (addendum) |
| idealized collision law | signal machines | Becker, Durand-Lose et al., ACM ToCT 2021 (addendum) |

The paper's existing relation sigma_1 ~ sigma_2 (same response distribution) is already this relation. With finitely
many histories under deterministic reversible dynamics, the minimal response partition always exists and computing it
is routine. "The shadow is an effective object because a compact state predicts its responses" is therefore **not a new
claim** and must not be the headline.

## What could be ours (results, each falsifiable)
1. **The physical description reaches the minimal partition.** The causal-state literature builds states from data and
   interprets them afterwards. Here the fields are fixed first (DESCRIBE-001's wall and domain language), and the test is
   whether the minimal response partition is generated by a subset of them. It could fail: the future might depend on
   something D carries only indirectly.
2. **Which fields.** The frozen prediction: wall position acts only as a space-time shift of the response; species and
   relative phase carry everything else.
3. **Held-out probes.** The transducer is defined over all inputs. Confirmation on unseen, wider probe species tests
   something finite data could break.
4. **A compositional law (strongest candidate).** Scattering tables measured on isolated walls predict whole-shadow
   responses. Signal machines are the idealized form, but as a model of computation, not a law extracted from a CA and
   checked against it.
5. **Setting.** A reversible substrate with exact bit accounting, where the object is a memory-bearing residue rather than
   a particle.

## Consequences for PREDICT-001
- Name the target in established terms: PREDICT-001 computes the epsilon-transducer (equivalently the Nerode partition)
  of each shadow over a finite probe set, and tests whether D's fields generate it and which ones.
- Put the weight on items 2 and 4 (PREDICT-002/003), not on "effective object".
- Cite Barnett & Crutchfield 2015, Shalizi & Crutchfield 2001, Shalizi et al. 2006 and Nerode 1958 in the paper.
