# Prior art for the reversible-CA shadow results (search log, 2026-11-04)
Method: web search plus Crossref keyword queries (Crossref was rate-limited after the first query).
No OpenAlex or Semantic Scholar key, so this is not systematic. Follow-up: a systematic search before submission.

## Directly overlapping
1. **Wolfram, NKS (2002), ch. 9, rule 37R.** Our rule 37 is exactly 37R (rule 37 XOR the cell two steps back).
   NKS reports localized structures, a background that repeats every 3 steps, long repetition periods,
   no ergodicity, and that "information on the initial conditions ends up being carried by localized
   structures". The existence of 37R's structures and its active vacuum is prior art.
2. **Lizier, Prokopenko & Zomaya (2008 PRE 77:026110; 2010 Chaos 20:037109; 2012 Inf. Sci. 208:39).**
   Local information dynamics in (irreversible) ECAs: gliders are the dominant information transfer
   entities, blinkers and *background domains are the dominant information storage*, and collisions are
   information modification. The idea that the background stores information is therefore established.
3. **Adamatzky, Martínez et al., collision-based computing** (Collision-Based Computing, Springer 2002;
   "Cellular automaton supercolliders" arXiv:1105.4332; Martínez, Adamatzky & Morita, J. Cell. Autom.
   13:325, 2018). Glider collision catalogues (fusion, elastic, soliton-like), ether backgrounds, stationary
   localizations as memory ("travelling data are re-written into stationary data"), and later gliders
   modified by those stationary structures. Mechanistic prior art for "a collision writes a record that a
   later glider reads".
4. **Hanson & Crutchfield (1992, 1997), computational mechanics:** particles as domain walls; domains and
   particles as intrinsic computation. Our bubble edges are domain walls between vacuum phases.

## Physics analogy (framing only, not cited as evidence)
- Gravitational memory effect / soft hair (Strominger; Hawking, Perry & Strominger 2016): a degenerate
  vacuum stores information after the carriers have gone.

## What appears not covered (to confirm in a systematic search)
- An *interventional* test, in a *reversible* substrate, that erases all resolved carriers and shows that
  the residual background alone fully determines (ratio 1.0) the information not carried away, as read
  by a later probe, with an exact null in the no-shadow rules.
- The exact information ledger (carriers versus background) over a complete rule family.
- Statistical measures (Lizier) and constructed gates (Adamatzky) do not do this erase-and-probe
  accounting. Narrow the novelty claim to this.
