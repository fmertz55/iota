## Finding that changes the PREDICT plan
The predict mapping note (2026-11-10) ranked the "rulebook" (isolated-wall tables predict whole-shadow responses) as
the strongest candidate, comparing it only with signal machines. That comparison was incomplete:
- **Factorized scattering is the defining property of integrable reversible CA.** In Rule 54 (buca2021), Rule 150
  (wilkinson2022), the Klobas-Prosen two-species RCA (klobas2022) and soliton CA (park1986; crystal-base scattering
  rules), multi-body outcomes are exactly products of two-body scatterings with time shifts. All are already cited in
  the shadow paper. A "rulebook that composes" is therefore prior art as a concept.
- In those systems the record of an interaction is the **time shift carried by the movers**. Nothing is left in the
  vacuum. LEDGER-001's "record travels relationally, as relative position" is this same known effect. Only the
  part of the record held by the medium (the shadow) is ours.
- Integrability breaking studied so far is stochastic (Lopez-Piqueres, Gopalakrishnan & Vasseur, J. Phys. A 2022,
  doi 10.1088/1751-8121/ac6b66: backscatter perturbation of Rule 54). Not found: deterministic, medium-held breaking.
- **Israeli & Goldenfeld, PRE 73:026203 (2006), doi 10.1103/physreve.73.026203**: coarse-graining CA via a commuting
  diagram (project, then evolve = evolve, then project). This is the CA precedent for the Neukart-Marx defect
  D = C∘Φ − Φ_eff∘C. **Not cited in the shadow paper.** Add at revision.
- Pivato, ETDS 2006 (doi 10.1017/s0143385706000599), abstract read: invariants of defects "partly explain the outcomes
  of their collisions". This overlaps BUDGET-000/001, not the composition question. Pivato 2007 (cited) has no
  OpenAlex abstract and was not read.

## Consequence (proposal, awaiting user OK)
Recast the strongest PREDICT result as a **composition test with an integrable positive control**. With movers
only, interactions compose exactly in integrable RCA (prior art). The new question is whether, in the shadow
rules, the composition defect is non-zero with movers only and returns to exactly zero once the medium's record
(the D description) is included as a partner. If so, the medium's record is precisely the missing term that lets
interactions compose.

## Self-check after approval (2026-11-15, before any contract): tautology hazard
Can COMPOSE-001 / PREDICT-001 fail? Mostly not.
- DESCRIBE-001 showed D rebuilds every cell of both layers. The rule is deterministic, local and translation
  invariant, and the vacuum is time-periodic. So two histories with equal D, up to a lattice shift and a time shift
  that is a multiple of the vacuum period, have identical cells. Any probe then gets an identical response, held-out
  probes included. PREDICT-001's "held-out probes" item therefore cannot fail inside D's validity window, and the
  11-10 assessment that it "can fail" was wrong. Only the minimal-field question has content, and the space shift
  is the expected part of it.
- COMPOSE-001 in the separated regime: with the residue kept as cells, sequential composition is exact by locality.
  "Movers only fails" is SHADOW-003 again. Rule 54 composing is known. Nothing in it can fail except the instrument.
- Content survives only where nothing guarantees the answer. Example: do two later interactions **commute** on a
  shadow? I.e., does the medium record the ORDER of events (bits of order held by the medium vs by the movers)?
  Integrable RCA are order-blind asymptotically (Yang-Baxter / additive shifts). 5 OpenAlex order/path-dependence
  queries (title screen) found no CA study measuring medium-held order memory.
