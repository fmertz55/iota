# Prior art for the reversible-CA shadow result

The shadow result sits where four established literatures meet, and each one takes away part of what might otherwise look new. What remains is narrower but, as far as a systematic OpenAlex sweep (30 keyword queries, then one citation step backward and forward from ten anchor papers) can tell, unclaimed: an *interventional* test, in an *information-conserving* law, that erases every resolved carrier after an interaction and shows that the residual background alone determines what a later coherence can do. That background holds exactly the information the carriers did not take away, and a probe recovers all of it. The test comes with an exact null in rules that leave no residue.

## What is already established

### Reversible substrates and their structures
Reversible, information-conserving dynamics and the second-order (Fredkin) construction we use go back to conservative logic and early invertible-automaton work ([Fredkin & Toffoli 1982](https://doi.org/10.1007/bf01857727); [Toffoli & Margolus 1987](https://doi.org/10.7551/mitpress/1763.001.0001); [Toffoli & Margolus 1990](https://doi.org/10.1016/0167-2789%2890%2990185-r)). Our rule 37 is Wolfram's rule 37R (A New Kind of Science, 2002, ch. 9; no DOI). NKS already reports its localized structures, its period-3 background and its non-ergodic fluctuation, so the existence of these objects is not a result of ours. A separate, mathematically sharper line treats specific reversible automata as exactly solvable many-body systems with interacting solitons. The reversible Rule 54 has quasiparticles that scatter with fixed time delays ([Buča et al. 2021](https://doi.org/10.1088/1742-5468/ac096b)), and related two-species and non-ergodic reversible automata have been solved ([Klobas & Prosen 2022](https://doi.org/10.1088/1751-8121/ac3ebc); [Prosen 2023](https://doi.org/10.3390/e25050739); [Wilkinson et al. 2022](https://doi.org/10.1103/physreve.105.034124)). That literature is the strongest prior art for *how* coherences interact in reversible rules. It analyses quasiparticle scattering and thermalization, not a record left in the background after the carriers are gone.

### Particles as information carriers
That coherent structures carry and process information is old. Soliton-like automata and phase-coded solitons were proposed for computation in the 1980s ([Park et al. 1986](https://doi.org/10.1016/0167-2789%2886%2990068-0); [Steiglitz et al. 1988](https://doi.org/10.1109/12.2143)), and particle-like structures and their interactions were catalogued soon after ([Boccara et al. 1991](https://doi.org/10.1103/physreva.44.866)). Computational mechanics made this formal: particles are defects between regular domains, and their interactions are the automaton's intrinsic computation ([Hanson & Crutchfield 1992](https://doi.org/10.1007/bf01054429); [Hanson & Crutchfield 1997](https://doi.org/10.1016/s0167-2789%2896%2900259-x); [Hordijk et al. 2001](https://doi.org/10.1016/s0167-2789%2801%2900252-4); [Pivato 2007](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tcs.2007.03.014); [Rupe & Crutchfield 2018](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.5021130)). Our bubble edges are domain walls in exactly this sense.

### The background as information storage
The closest conceptual precedent is the local information-dynamics programme. In ordinary, irreversible elementary automata, local transfer entropy singles out gliders as the transfer entities ([Lizier et al. 2008](https://doi.org/10.1103/physreve.77.026110)), collisions as information modification ([Lizier et al. 2010](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.3486801)), and blinkers and *background domains* as the dominant information storage ([Lizier et al. 2012](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ins.2012.04.016)). "The background stores information" is therefore established and must be cited as such. These measures are statistical: they are computed over ensembles of runs, and transfer entropy is known not to equal causal influence ([Lizier & Prokopenko 2010](https://doi.org/10.1140/epjb/e2010-00034-5); [James et al. 2016](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevlett.116.238701)). The interventional alternative, information flow defined by do-style interventions on a causal network, exists ([Ay & Polani 2008](https://doi.org/10.1142/s0219525908001465)), but we found no application that erases carriers and probes the residue in a cellular automaton.

### Collisions that write memory
Collision-based computing builds logic from glider collisions. It catalogues fusion, elastic and soliton-like outcomes, ether backgrounds, and stationary localizations that act as memory and alter later gliders ([Adamatzky & Durand-Lose 2012](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-540-92910-9_58); [Martínez et al. 2006](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.chaos.2005.05.013); [Martínez et al. 2011](https://doi.org/10.1142/s0129183111016348); [Martínez et al. 2012](https://doi.org/10.25088/complexsystems.21.2.117)). This is the main mechanistic precedent for "a collision writes something that a later glider reads". It differs in kind: the written record there is a designed, localized object in irreversible rules, used as a gate, rather than an emergent vacuum-phase domain in a reversible rule measured with an exact information ledger. Outside automata, the idea that an agent leaves information in its environment that later constrains behaviour has an information-theoretic treatment as stigmergy ([Klyubin et al. 2004](https://doi.org/10.7551/mitpress/1429.003.0095)) and, in biology, as outsourced memory through niche construction ([Lee et al. 2022](https://doi.org/10.1101/2022.09.01.506204), preprint). Both are relevant framing for ICP, though neither works in a reversible substrate.

## What appears unclaimed

Four elements of our work did not surface in any of the above or in their one-step citation neighbourhoods:
1. carrier erasure as the intervention;
2. an exact carrier-versus-background information ledger over a complete rule family, made possible because the law is reversible;
3. the finding that the background's share is fully recoverable by a probe (ratio 1.0 in 45 of 45 shadow pairs), and often carried out by the probe coherence itself;
4. an exact zero in every rule whose background returns to vacuum.

These are the claims the Complex Systems paper should make, positioned explicitly against Lizier et al. (statistical storage), Adamatzky and Martínez (designed collision memory) and the reversible Rule 54 programme (quasiparticle scattering).

## Limits of this search
OpenAlex keyword retrieval was noisy, so recall rests mainly on the citation steps. NKS has no DOI and was not traversed. Semantic Scholar was not queried. Two remaining risks are worth a targeted read before submission. First, any work on information flow under interventions in reversible automata. Second, papers citing Klyubin et al. that use cellular automata. The anchors' forward neighbourhoods contained none.
