\begin{abstract}
Information is investigated as an interaction-induced change in reachable
future states. For uniform laws on nested reachable supports, the logarithmic
support-ratio measure is exactly a Kullback--Leibler relative entropy. This
identity motivates a policy- and horizon-indexed future-law divergence and a
prospective bridge to the geometric quantum relative entropy used in Gravity
from Entropy (GfE). A finite local lattice system is used to test whether
relational constraints can persist when the local cells realizing them are
replaced.
Three strict constraint-transport events show tracked regions disconnecting
after bridge loss and reconnecting through a causally necessary disjoint bridge
under payload-free dynamics. Prospective ancestry then shows expression lineage
surviving stationary-address death. In 192 fresh branches, one-decision
interventions reopened all 36 targeted successor routes; all six gate
interventions produced immediate children and 28 of 30 exhaustion interventions
eventually handed off. A complete screen of 16 unused initialization seeds froze
two new geometries before terminal outcomes; the same protocol rescued all eight
new gate-failure cases and produced directionally correct exhaustion and
preemption mediators. The result is bounded causal evidence that a lawful
successor must remain available and be realized before closure. A separately
committed reverse intervention vetoed 164 natural unique-child events: 122
branches realized an alternate child and handed off, while 42 realized none and
lost the lineage. Extending those branches through prospectively frozen
sequential vetoes produced an observed ordered cut in \GCTenCutTotal{} of
\GCTenInitial{} branches by depth eight; \GCTenAboveMax{} remained above the
protocol maximum and \GCTenSourceCensored{} retained the original source at the
fixed horizon. A preregistered independent replication then screened new
initializations before outcomes, selected \GCElevenSources{} geometries, and
repeated the policy on \GCElevenInitial{} factual continuations. All four
geometries retained a continuation after the first veto; \GCElevenCutTotal{}
branches cut by depth eight, \GCElevenAboveMax{} remained above the cap, and
\GCElevenSourceCensored{} was source-survival censored. These are bounded
simulator-level causal results, not a globally minimal cut or a claim of
physical correspondence. The distributional diagnostic and GfE mapping are
formal research proposals; neither is tested by the reported events.
\end{abstract}
