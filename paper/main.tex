\begin{abstract}
Information is investigated as an interaction-induced change in reachable
future states. A finite local lattice system is used to test whether relational
constraints can persist when the local cells realizing them are replaced. The
current evidence consists of three strict constraint-transport events across
two seeds. In each event, tracked regions disconnect after loss of a
role-bearing bridge and reconnect through a causally necessary, disjoint
successor bridge under payload-free, no-stored-phase dynamics. These results
establish a bounded computational observation rather than a claim of physical
correspondence. The repository exposes the definitions, claim registry, source
artifacts, analysis, and generated manuscript values required to audit the
result.
\end{abstract}
