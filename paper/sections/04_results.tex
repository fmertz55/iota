\subsection{Held-out compact-rule test}

Finding 064 trained a preregistered decision stump on 48 terminal-aligned
branches and evaluated it once on 48 held-out branches. The selected rule was
singleton-lineage size greater than $1.5$ immediately before original-address
death. Held-out sensitivity was \GCFiveSensitivity{}, specificity was
\GCFiveSpecificity{}, and balanced accuracy was \GCFiveBalancedAccuracy{}

missed 23 of 36 handoffs; it is therefore treated as a sufficient state marker,
not a general continuation law.

\subsection{Ordered terminal events and a causal continuation gate}

Finding 066 instrumented every selected hard-transfer candidate on the terminal
tick for 96 fresh branches. The observer passed exact state-and-RNG equivalence
and recorded 20,748 terminal candidate events. The ordered ledger classified all
96 outcomes without collisions: 57 death-tick child activations, 16
preexisting-descendant continuations, 19 source-exhaustion deaths, three
transfer-gate deaths, and one target-preemption death. In a preregistered replay
of one gate-failure branch, permitting exactly one failed successor gate while
still consuming its original random draw changed the outcome from lineage death
to one collision-free ancestry-preserving child. This supports a bounded causal
claim: continuation can depend on realizing an eligible successor before the
source expends its terminating amplitude.

\subsection{Prospective causal replication and independent geometries}

Finding 067 froze three one-decision policies before evaluating 192 fresh
branches from the original source states. Permitting the first failed gate to an
inactive successor produced an immediate inherited child in
\GCEightSameGateRescues{}/\GCEightSameGateCases{} cases. Vetoing the successful
non-child transfer that would spend the final source amplitude reopened the
route in \GCEightSameExhaustRoutes{}/\GCEightSameExhaustCases{} cases; three
formed a child immediately and \GCEightSameExhaustHandoffs{} eventually handed
off. Exact matched shams, one-event matching, payload absence, conservation, and
collision freedom held throughout.

A complete screen of \GCEightScreenCases{} continuations from 16 unused
initialization seeds found four singleton-eligible strict transports across two
seeds and froze one primary source per seed before terminal outcomes. In 64
branches from these \GCEightIndependentSources{} independent geometries, seed 71
produced eight successor-gate deaths; all eight interventions produced immediate
children and handoff. One seed-58 exhaustion intervention reopened the route but
did not eventually hand off, while one seed-71 preemption intervention restored
target availability and was followed by handoff four offsets later. These
results separate two causal conditions: route availability and successor
realization. Reopening opportunity is causal, but it is not by itself sufficient
for continuation.

\subsection{Necessity and temporal redundancy of realization}

Finding 068 completed 256 fresh factual branches and committed
\GCNineEligible{} unique-child selectors before any veto outcome. Suppressing
the selected child produced an alternate child on the same tick in
\GCNineSameTick{} branches, after a delay in \GCNineDelayed{}, and never in
\GCNineNoAlternate{}. All \GCNineHandoffs{} branches with an alternate child
handed off at first address death; all \GCNineLosses{} branches without one lost
the lineage. No source-survival endpoint was censored.

The selected target was the first alternate target in only
\GCNineFirstSameTarget{} cases; \GCNineFirstDifferentTarget{} alternatives used
a different address. Source capacity was retained immediately after veto in
\GCNineCapacityRetained{}/\GCNineEligible{} branches. Both temporal redundancy
and branch-level necessity occurred in every represented initialization seed.
All selected events were suppressed, all shams and selector identities were
exact through the original draw, and no collision, payload, or conservation
failure occurred.
