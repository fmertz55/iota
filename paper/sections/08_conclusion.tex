\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

In this finite local model, a relational connectivity role survived complete
replacement of its original bridge, and an expression lineage survived loss of
its initial address through lawful successor realization. Paired gate,
exhaustion, preemption, and reverse-veto interventions show that continuation
depends on both route availability and timely realization. Repeated vetoes and
an independent four-geometry cohort further show that counterfactual redundancy
can recur along changed histories rather than residing in one static reserve.

The result is deliberately bounded. It demonstrates causal constraint transport
and history-dependent successor accessibility inside a frozen stochastic
simulator. It does not demonstrate an unprestatable adjacent possible,
open-ended evolution, substrate independence, or emergent gravity. The signed
support deformation $\chi$ and its pruning--enabling KL duality provide a
prospective way to test those future-space changes without conflating
contraction and expansion. The separately registered Dirac-QCA construction
shows that the same prospective method can survive a defect audit with a
narrowed cross-domain claim; it does not extend the present biological result.
The decisive next experiment is therefore not
another retrospective analogy: it is a preregistered carrier intervention that
predicts enabled, lost, and retained future identities and a held-out
micro-pruning to role-enabling crossover beyond complete native models.
