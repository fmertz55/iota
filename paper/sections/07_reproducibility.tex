\section{Reproducibility and Evidence}
\label{sec:reproducibility}

The paper uses generated rather than manually copied numbers. Canonical claims
and definitions are in
\path{evidence/claims.yaml} and \path{evidence/definitions.yaml}. Each
\path{evidence/manifests/GC-NNN.json} records the exact path, byte count, and
SHA-256 digest of supporting evidence and code; raw artifacts remain under
\path{output/findings/}.

Finding 069's cases, prospective selector manifests, and aggregate tables are
in \path{output/findings/finding069/}; its claim manifest identifies both
implementations. Before reporting cut assignments, the synthesis verifies
selector and parent-chain hashes, contiguous depths, nested risk sets, event
identities, and safety invariants.

\texttt{make verify} rebuilds the eight-stage synthesis, regenerates derived
files, and validates expectations, provenance, paths, and freshness.
\texttt{make paper} then compiles this PDF.
