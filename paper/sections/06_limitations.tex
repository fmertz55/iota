\section{Limitations and Falsification Frontier}
\label{sec:limitations}

The structural foundation remains three strict events across two original
initialization seeds, all preserving a large-region/singleton relation. The
independent screen adds two primary geometries for the one-decision mediator
tests, but only one contains a replicated gate-failure cohort of at least five
cases. The independent exhaustion and preemption cohorts contain one case each
and support only case-level mediator claims.

The ledger covers candidates selected by the declared hard-transfer routine.
Candidate selection and lexicographic source scheduling are not yet separately
intervened upon and may be implementation-specific. Forcing a failed gate to
pass is an efficacy test, not an estimate of its natural probability. The
original draw is consumed and paired worlds are identical through intervention,
but later random-number consumption can diverge after their states diverge.

The reverse-veto cohort conditions on factual natural continuation and therefore
estimates robustness, not the prevalence of necessity across all branches.
Vetoing a successful child retains source amplitude and intentionally exposes
alternate routes. Inherited realization is part of the operational lineage
definition, so the exact alternate-child/handoff correspondence is not an
independent predictive association. Five source states represent four
initialization seeds.

Sequential depth is prefix-minimal only for the frozen first-next-child policy.
Because each suppressed transfer leaves amplitude at its source, successive
counterfactual worlds do not expose a fixed list of factual routes. The result
is neither a graph-theoretic minimum cut nor evidence that each earlier prefix
member is individually necessary. The protocol also truncates two axes:
right-censoring above depth eight and source-survival censoring at the common
follow-up endpoint. Independent replication remains conditional on factual
unique-child continuation and supports recurrence, not a scheduler-invariant
topology.

The adjacent-possible interpretation is prospective. No outcome-type map,
actualized repertoire, complete reachable support, $E/L/K$ ledger,
$\chi$, or adjacent frontier was estimated in the reported cohorts. Alternate
addresses need not be alternate types. The selected branches are censored and
policy-dependent, sequential vetoes retain amplitude and alter history, and the
finite law and alphabet cannot demonstrate open-ended phase-space creation.
Even when measured, $\chi$ alone can miss complete identity turnover in
nonnested supports.

Indefinite persistence, iterated strict transport, a necessary-and-sufficient
continuation law, uniqueness to this update law, and physical correspondence
remain unestablished. Further falsification should extend the frozen horizon and
depth, randomize or compare selector policies and source schedules, fit complete
state and native adjacent-possible baselines, and enumerate small intervention
subsets on held-out branches. Mass, energy, force, matter, and physical-field
correspondence remain downstream hypotheses rather than demonstrated substrate
primitives.

The relative-entropy relations in
Equations~\ref{eq:future-law-divergence} and \ref{eq:signed-kl-duality} are
mathematical diagnostics, not empirical validation of the organizing
hypothesis. The GfE map adds further untested requirements: positive operators
on a common space, a derived metric and volume interpretation, units,
normalization, sector control, covariance, functional variation, a continuum
limit, and robustness across laws, schedulers, topologies, boundaries, and
representations. No GfE scaling, gravitational transition, or observational
signature has been derived or tested by this simulator. A future geometric
claim must also distinguish the signed carrier response from the existing
Fisher-volume $\Lambda_B$ construction.
