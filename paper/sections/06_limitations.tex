\section{Limitations and Falsification Frontier}
\label{sec:limitations}

The structural foundation remains three strict Finding 060 events across two
original seeds, all preserving a large-region/singleton relation. Finding 067's
independent screen adds two primary initialization geometries, but only one
contains a replicated gate-failure cohort of at least five cases. The independent
exhaustion and preemption cohorts contain one case each and support only
case-level mediator claims.

The ledger covers candidates selected by the declared hard-transfer routine;
candidate selection and lexicographic source scheduling are not yet separately
intervened upon and may be implementation-specific. Forcing a failed gate to
pass is an efficacy test, not an estimate of natural probability. The original
random draw is consumed and paired worlds are identical through intervention,
but later RNG consumption can diverge after their states diverge.

Finding 068 conditions on factual natural continuation and therefore estimates
its robustness, not the prevalence of necessity across all branches. Vetoing a
successful child retains source amplitude and intentionally exposes alternate
routes. Moreover, inherited realization is part of the operational lineage
definition, so the exact alternate-child/handoff correspondence is not an
independent predictive association. Five source states represent four
initialization seeds.

Finding 069 reports a prefix-minimal depth only for the frozen
first-next-child policy. Because each suppressed transfer leaves amplitude at
its source, successive counterfactual worlds do not expose a fixed list of
routes present in the factual world. The result is therefore neither a
graph-theoretic minimum cut nor evidence that every earlier member of a cutting
prefix is individually necessary. The protocol also truncates two axes:
\GCTenAboveMax{} branches are right-censored above depth eight, and
\GCTenSourceCensored{} are source-survival censored at the common
\GCTenFixedHorizon{}-offset endpoint. Their cut depths are unknown.

Finding 070 independently replicates the policy response in four new source
geometries, but its 79 branches remain conditional on factual unique-child
continuation. Ten are depth-right-censored and one is source-survival censored;
one cut includes a transient child that died before the endpoint. The
replication therefore supports recurrence, not a scheduler-invariant topology,
an entropy interpretation, or an exhaustive inventory of reachable futures.

Finding 074 is conditional on 25 strict singleton transports with an eligible
depleted-target activation of the future articulation during the preceding 64
offsets. Its primary endpoint is the component split at the frozen natural
offset, not prevention of every later separation. The memory intervention
erases $R$, $V$, $P$, and angular memory as a bundle, so the necessary state
coordinate in seven sensitive channels is unresolved. The transplant recipient
was selected from its exact pre-step state, but ordinary update dynamics could
change recipient memory on the intervention tick before the declared post-state
move. Finally, rebridge veto never met the 128-offset durability gate.

Indefinite persistence, iterated strict transport, a necessary-and-sufficient
continuation law, uniqueness to this update law, and physical correspondence
remain unestablished. Further falsification should extend the frozen horizon and
depth, compare alternative selector policies and source schedules, and
enumerate small intervention subsets on held-out branches.
Mass, energy, force, matter, and physical-field correspondence remain downstream
hypotheses rather than demonstrated substrate primitives.
