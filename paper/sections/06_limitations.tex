\section{Limitations and Falsification Frontier}
\label{sec:limitations}

Only three of 36 Class B events passed the strict transport criterion. The
positives occur across two seeds and preserve a large-region/singleton relation;
they do not yet demonstrate a general multi-region invariant. Indefinite
persistence, iterated transport, uniqueness to a particular law, and physical
correspondence have not been established.

The next gate tracks both predecessor-region descendants after the strict
positives, measures the restored relation's lifetime, tests for a second bridge
replacement preserving the relation, and compares the positives against the
nine role-bearing failures. A lineage failure or loss of the asserted causal
role would narrow or falsify the current interpretation.
