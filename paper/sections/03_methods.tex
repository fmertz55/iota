\section{Constraint-Transport Test}
\label{sec:methods}

Finding 060 replayed the 36 Class B bridge-relocation events identified by the
preceding class-matrix experiment. The audit required a role-bearing predecessor
bridge and tracked the exact predecessor regions through bridge loss and
reconnection. Pairwise reachability and shortest-path accessibility were
measured before loss, during the split, and after rebridge. The successor bridge
was then removed to test whether it was causally necessary.

Let $C_{UV}(t)$ equal one when tracked regions $U_t$ and $V_t$ are mutually
reachable and zero otherwise. A strict event requires

\begin{equation}
C_{UV}(t_{\mathrm{pre}})=1,\qquad
C_{UV}(t_{\mathrm{split}})=0,\qquad
C_{UV}(t_{\mathrm{post}})=1,
\end{equation}

together with disjoint predecessor and successor bridges,
$B_{\mathrm{pre}}\cap B_{\mathrm{post}}=\varnothing$, and loss of restored
reachability after removing $B_{\mathrm{post}}$.

The analysis code and exact source tables are linked from
Section~\ref{sec:reproducibility}.

\subsection{Expression ancestry and terminal-event intervention}

A prospective expression lineage retains an active address and inherits a newly
activated target only from a successful transfer whose source belonged to that
lineage before the update. Already-active targets do not inherit identity,
terminated lineages do not resurrect, and competing claims to one child mark a
collision.

For Finding 067, a default-off observer recorded selected transfer candidates in
evaluation order, including target state, transfer probability, random draw,
and effective decision. Paired observer-free and sham-intervention worlds were
required to remain exactly equal in every state array and RNG state. Each causal
replay consumed the natural random draw but changed exactly one decision:
permit a failed inactive-successor gate, veto the non-child transfer spending
the source's final amplitude, or veto the earlier activation preempting a target.
The primary mediators were route reopening and immediate inherited-child
creation; eventual handoff at first address death was evaluated separately.

\subsection{Independent-geometry holdout}

The independent screen exhausted a frozen $16\times8$ matrix: unused
initialization seeds 57--72 and eight distinct continuation streams per seed.
Every Class B candidate received the unchanged strict transport audit. Before
terminal outcomes were examined, the source manifest retained at most the
lexicographically first eligible singleton transport per initialization seed.
Each selected source was then branched 32 times at rebridge, rather than at a
later terminal-aligned state, and evaluated with the same intervention policy.
Initialization seed is the independent geometry unit; branch outcomes are
conditional stochastic replicates.

\subsection{Reverse necessity and redundancy test}

Finding 068 separated factual selection from intervention. First, all 256 fresh
factual branches were completed and every collision-free, single-address,
natural child event was written to a selector manifest. The manifest stored the
candidate and gate ordinals, source, target, amplitude, target state,
probability, and random draw and was committed before replay. Every selected
factual event had exactly one inherited child.

The replay stage accepted only the committed manifest hash. It reproduced an
exact sham, matched every frozen event field, consumed the natural draw, and
forced that one successful child gate to fail. Each branch was followed for 128
offsets after intervention or until first original-address death. Alternate
child timing (same tick, delayed, or none) and address endpoint (handoff, lineage
loss, or source-survival censoring) were recorded as independent axes.

\subsection{Sequential retained-amplitude cut protocol}

Finding 069 extended the committed Finding 068 cohort with a staged sequence of
at most eight vetoes. At depth $d$, the complete selector prefix through
$d-1$ was replayed from the common branch state. For every branch that still
handed off, the first next ancestry-preserving child was joined to its successful
candidate-ledger row. Its tick and gate ordinal, source, target, candidate
step and order, available amplitude, target state, probability, and random draw
were written to a complete manifest and hashed and committed before the depth-$d$
gate was vetoed. Every later replay had to reproduce all earlier selector
identities and natural gate passes exactly. A veto consumed the frozen random
draw but suppressed the transfer, thereby retaining at the source the amplitude
that the natural realization would have spent.

Let $H_b^{(d)}=1$ when branch $b$ retains a collision-free descendant at first
original-address death after the first $d$ committed vetoes, and let
$H_b^{(d)}=0$ when that address dies without one. The measured depth is

\begin{equation}
D_b=\min\{d\in\{1,\ldots,8\}:H_b^{(d)}=0\}.
\end{equation}

This is a prefix-minimal depth under the frozen first-next-child and
retained-amplitude policy. It is not a minimum over arbitrary event subsets or
state interventions. Every stage used the same endpoint window:
\GCTenFixedHorizon{} offsets after the depth-1 intervention. A depth-8 handoff
was right-censored above the protocol maximum; an original address still active
at the fixed horizon was separately source-survival censored. Collision,
payload, or conservation failures were protocol failures, not post-treatment
exclusions.

\subsection{Independent ordered-cut replication}

Finding 070 froze a 256-case screen from 32 previously unused initialization
seeds before continuation outcomes. The lexicographically first strict eligible
source per seed yielded four source geometries. Thirty-two factual branches per
source were completed, and every collision-free natural continuation with
exactly one inherited child was committed before any veto. The identical
Finding 069 selector rule, retained-amplitude intervention, 128-offset endpoint,
and depth-eight cap were then applied. Replication was preregistered as at least
five eligible selectors per source and a depth-1 handoff in at least two newly
selected sources; exact depth-1 matched shams and the full selector-hash chain
remained required protocol checks.
