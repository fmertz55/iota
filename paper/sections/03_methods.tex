\section{Constraint-Transport Test}
\label{sec:methods}

Finding 060 replayed the 36 Class B bridge-relocation events identified by the
preceding class-matrix experiment. The audit required a role-bearing predecessor
bridge and tracked the exact predecessor regions through bridge loss and
reconnection. Pairwise reachability and shortest-path accessibility were
measured before loss, during the split, and after rebridge. The successor bridge
was then removed to test whether it was causally necessary.

Let $C_{UV}(t)$ equal one when tracked regions $U_t$ and $V_t$ are mutually
reachable and zero otherwise. A strict event requires

\begin{equation}
C_{UV}(t_{\mathrm{pre}})=1,\qquad
C_{UV}(t_{\mathrm{split}})=0,\qquad
C_{UV}(t_{\mathrm{post}})=1,
\end{equation}

together with disjoint predecessor and successor bridges,
$B_{\mathrm{pre}}\cap B_{\mathrm{post}}=\varnothing$, and loss of restored
reachability after removing $B_{\mathrm{post}}$.

The analysis code and exact source tables are linked from
Section~\ref{sec:reproducibility}.
