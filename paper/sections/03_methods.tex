\section{Constraint-Transport Test}
\label{sec:methods}

Finding 060 replayed the 36 Class B bridge-relocation events identified by the
preceding class-matrix experiment. The audit required a role-bearing predecessor
bridge and tracked the exact predecessor regions through bridge loss and
reconnection. Pairwise reachability and shortest-path accessibility were
measured before loss, during the split, and after rebridge. The successor bridge
was then removed to test whether it was causally necessary.

Let $C_{UV}(t)$ equal one when tracked regions $U_t$ and $V_t$ are mutually
reachable and zero otherwise. A strict event requires

\begin{equation}
C_{UV}(t_{\mathrm{pre}})=1,\qquad
C_{UV}(t_{\mathrm{split}})=0,\qquad
C_{UV}(t_{\mathrm{post}})=1,
\end{equation}

together with disjoint predecessor and successor bridges,
$B_{\mathrm{pre}}\cap B_{\mathrm{post}}=\varnothing$, and loss of restored
reachability after removing $B_{\mathrm{post}}$.

The analysis code and exact source tables are linked from
Section~\ref{sec:reproducibility}.

\subsection{Expression ancestry and terminal-event intervention}

A prospective expression lineage retains an active address and inherits a newly
activated target only from a successful transfer whose source belonged to that
lineage before the update. Already-active targets do not inherit identity,
terminated lineages do not resurrect, and competing claims to one child mark a
collision.

For Finding 067, a default-off observer recorded selected transfer candidates in
evaluation order, including target state, transfer probability, random draw,
and effective decision. Paired observer-free and sham-intervention worlds were
required to remain exactly equal in every state array and RNG state. Each causal
replay consumed the natural random draw but changed exactly one decision:
permit a failed inactive-successor gate, veto the non-child transfer spending
the source's final amplitude, or veto the earlier activation preempting a target.
The primary mediators were route reopening and immediate inherited-child
creation; eventual handoff at first address death was evaluated separately.

\subsection{Independent-geometry holdout}

The independent screen exhausted a frozen $16\times8$ matrix: unused
initialization seeds 57--72 and eight distinct continuation streams per seed.
Every Class B candidate received the unchanged strict transport audit. Before
terminal outcomes were examined, the source manifest retained at most the
lexicographically first eligible singleton transport per initialization seed.
Each selected source was then branched 32 times at rebridge, rather than at a
later terminal-aligned state, and evaluated with the same intervention policy.
Initialization seed is the independent geometry unit; branch outcomes are
conditional stochastic replicates.
