\section{Provisional Model}
\label{sec:model}

At permanent lattice address $x$, write an iota provisionally as

\begin{equation}
I_x(t)=\left(x,A_x(t),E_x(t)\right),
\end{equation}

where $A_x(t)$ is its attribute state and $E_x(t)$ is its expression state. The
26-cell Moore neighborhood is denoted by $N_{26}(x,t)$. A fixed local law
$\mathcal{L}$ defines the generative kernel

\begin{equation}
\left(A_x(t+1),E_x(t+1)\right)
=
\mathcal{L}\left(A_x(t),E_x(t),N_{26}(x,t)\right).
\label{eq:kernel}
\end{equation}

Equation~\ref{eq:kernel} is a model definition. No identification with a
fundamental physical particle is assumed.
