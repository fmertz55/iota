\section{Finite Local Model}
\label{sec:model}

At permanent lattice address $x$, write an iota provisionally as

\begin{equation}
I_x(t)=\left(x,A_x(t),E_x(t)\right),
\end{equation}

where $A_x(t)$ is its integer amplitude and $E_x(t)$ collects the remaining
local expression coordinates. The experiments use a $64^3$ periodic lattice.
The endogenous implementation state comprises the arrays
\texttt{active}, $A$, $R$, $V$, $P$, \texttt{latent\_A}, angular memory $L$,
and previous-activity bookkeeping. A fixed local law $\mathcal{L}$, fixed
scheduler, and pseudorandom generator define the stochastic kernel

\begin{equation}
S_{t+1}\sim\mathcal{L}(S_t,\xi_t;\vartheta),
\label{eq:kernel}
\end{equation}

where $\xi_t$ is the declared exogenous random stream and $\vartheta$ is the
frozen parameter vector. Equation~\ref{eq:kernel} is a model definition. No
identification with a fundamental physical particle is assumed.

The update is ordered rather than a purely synchronous cellular-automaton map.
Tick-start active sources are visited in lexicographic array order. A source's
primary vector target and scored local candidates are then considered in a
deterministic candidate order. Source fields are read from the tick-start state,
while target eligibility and remaining source amplitude may reflect earlier
events in the same tick. Random gate draws are consumed sequentially. These
scheduler semantics are part of the law and are held fixed in every paired
intervention.

Each successful hard transfer debits one amplitude quantum from its source and
credits the target. An inactive target with latent budget activates by combining
that budget with the transferred quantum; an eligible depleted target may be
recruited only through the declared residual channel. A gate with effective
influence $q$ succeeds with probability
$\min(1,k_Aq)$; failure changes no amplitude state. At tick end, a source whose
amplitude is exhausted deactivates. The integer ledger

\begin{equation}
\sum_{x:\,\mathrm{active}} A_x(t)
+\sum_{x:\,\mathrm{inactive}}\mathrm{latent\_A}_x(t)
=A_{\mathrm{total}}
\label{eq:amplitude-ledger}
\end{equation}

is required to remain exact. Payload and vector-label updates are conditional
on successful exchange and do not alter this ledger.

The principal experiments use the versioned v0.3.6 component-bridge
configuration. Table~\ref{tab:model-parameters} reports its principal values
and is generated directly from the complete canonical YAML file under
\path{output/findings/configs/}, so manuscript values cannot drift from the
executable configuration.
