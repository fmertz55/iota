# Iota Rig

[![Research compendium](https://github.com/fmertz55/iota/actions/workflows/research-compendium.yml/badge.svg)](https://github.com/fmertz55/iota/actions/workflows/research-compendium.yml)

> **Organizing hypothesis**
>
> **Information is the change in the reachable caused by interaction.**

Iota Rig is an open, automation-first experimental program for an
information-first, finite-local approach to physics. Its current stochastic
lattice implementation is a candidate computational research model, not an
established physical substrate or living system. The BioSystems manuscript uses
it as an artificial-life application focused on organizational persistence.
The repository is both the lab and the live record of the investigation:
definitions, laws, experiments, negative results, evidence, and interpretations
are intended to evolve together without erasing their history.

For this program, that hypothesis has complementary condition and change
forms:

> **Informational condition:** The informational condition of a lattice at
> time $t$ is the causally retained realization of its interaction history:
> its current local states and active relational topology, together with the
> constraints they impose on future trajectories reachable under admissible
> interventions.
>
> **Informational change:** Information changes when an interaction changes
> that reachable-future structure.

In compact form,

$$
H_{\leq t}\longrightarrow (X_t,G_t)
\xrightarrow{\mathcal{L},\,a}\mathcal{R}_t(a),
$$

where $H_{\leq t}$ is realized interaction history, $X_t$ is current local
state, $G_t$ is active relational topology, and $\mathcal{R}_t(a)$ is the set
or distribution of future trajectories reachable under admissible
intervention $a$. Only causally retained history counts as present information:
histories that leave no distinction in current state, relational topology, or
future intervention response are operationally equivalent at that time. Fixed
topology, laws, scheduling, and constants remain declared and counted
construction structure; dynamic topology may itself retain history.

The hypothesis is not presented as an established law of physics. It is the root
question from which the experiments are derived:

> Can finite local interactions produce constraints on reachable futures that
> persist even when the local state that realizes them is replaced?

### Quantum-Dirac construction result

Two separately frozen local update specifications—a product walk and the
established direct symmetry-derived QCA—recorded positive development and
single-use confirmation classifications for the same bounded `1+1D` Dirac
role core. Their uniform evolution bounds, observable witnesses, causal and
symmetry checks, and pre-outcome analytic leakage bounds remain supported.

A hostile post-result audit also found a shared factor-of-two error in the
direct QCA's empirical pi-sector predicate: it selected an empty sector in both
the primary and replay implementations. The measured-zero records and
rough-potential zero are withdrawn; the content-addressed ledgers are preserved and
the defect is documented. The valid analytic leakage bound used the correct
sector boundary and remains far below its frozen gate.

The two one-excitation free laws are exactly related by a half-cell time-frame
conjugacy after mass-angle remapping, and their declared resource difference
depends on nonminimal gate-fusion conventions. The product mass mixer's
full-space collision phase was not frozen. The intact evidence therefore
supports a qualified constructive-sufficiency claim for the shared bounded
role core across two registered update specifications, not the complete
originally registered direct role, architecture-class breadth, algebraically
independent dynamics, or a resource advantage.

### Minimal realization objective

The program is not searching for physics that differs from current physics. It
is searching for the smallest closed informational construction whose reachable
futures and causal roles agree with a declared domain of current physics.

Candidates are ranked by the frozen tuple

```text
(normalized informational primitives, atomic laws, elementary ticks to proto-object)
```

Fewer primitives wins first. With equal primitive count, fewer laws wins. Ticks
break a tie only when both earlier counts are equal. The current pilot reports
dimensionless elementary causal layers; it makes no Planck-time or other
physical-time conversion without a separately recovered clock map.

No law may be compound. A law must be self-contained, govern one degree of
freedom, and contain no `and`, `but`, or `or` clause. Hidden state, helper logic,
branches, lookup entries, and assumed continuants are expanded into counted
primitives or laws.

The controlling grammar, proto-object definition, accounting rules, data policy,
and physical-role criterion are in the
[ICP Minimal Informational Realization Contract](docs/ICP_MINIMAL_INFORMATIONAL_REALIZATION_CONTRACT.md).
Its current `0.2-draft` reference program begins at `N_P=2`: one binary event
channel plus one charged interaction-relation channel. The bounded pilot found
no complete proto-object by `H=16`. A separately frozen, outcome-aware
continuation then followed the same 15 nonempty canonical trajectories until
first pass or exact post-cone recurrence under the versioned
`MIR-ISOLATED-DYAD-OUTWARD-EFFECT-EXACT-ORBIT-V0.1` temporal extension.

All 15 nonempty rules recurred exactly without passing; the empty rule remains
analytically excluded. Recurrence certificates closed by layer 318, so no
candidate reached the one-million-layer resource ceiling and there is no
winner. Only two rules ever formed the attributed dyad, both at layer 1, and
neither ever produced an eligible one-tick successor. Thus no rule in this
complete frozen production-row family produces the registered exact-orbit
proto-object from the canonical seed on `C64` at any finite time. This is an
exact fixed-family falsification—not an exclusion of other `N_P=2` grammars,
other seeds or topologies, a general object impossibility, or global
minimality. Only origin `0` was evaluated; reserved offsets `48..63` remain
untouched. See the [exact-orbit contract](docs/ICP_MINIMAL_INFORMATIONAL_REALIZATION_EXACT_ORBIT_CONTRACT.md),
[canonical result](output/minimal_realization/MIR-EXACT-ORBIT-001/exact_orbit_results.json),
and [post-result interpretation](docs/literature/minimal_realization_exact_orbit_result_followup_2026-07-13.md).

### Research-program guardrail

The primitive target is the **latent counterfactual structure** of lawful
successor events, not an early imitation of matter, mass, energy, force, or a
classical field. The lattice stores state; its marked relational transition
graph represents which interactions remain possible; coherence is provisionally
the organization that preserves and transmits part of that successor structure
across realized transitions.

Every proposed experiment must therefore answer:

> Does this test measure a coherence's causal transformation of possible
> successor transitions, or only search for the appearance of a downstream
> physical observable?

Tests aimed primarily at attraction, radial decay, energy flow, particle motion,
or other familiar observables remain downstream unless their counterfactual
mechanism has already been demonstrated. The working explanatory order is:

1. persistent sites retain state;
2. lawful relations permit or exclude exchanges;
3. the present defines a latent graph of realizable successor events;
4. coherence preserves selected successor structure across transitions;
5. inheritance passes a modified set of future affordances onward;
6. recurrent organizations may yield stable higher-order invariants; and
7. mass- or energy-like behavior, if it emerges, is a coarse-grained output to
   explain rather than a primitive variable to imitate.

The immediate research target is **causal transformation of the successor graph
by a coherence**. “Coherence-conditioned transition geometry” is the working
term for that target; it does not assert a physical field or correspondence with
known matter.

## From hypothesis to experiment

At permanent lattice address $x$, write an iota provisionally as

$$
I_x(t) = \left(x, A_x(t), E_x(t)\right),
$$

where $A_x(t)$ is its attribute state, $E_x(t)$ is its expression state, and
$N_{26}(x,t)$ is its 26-neighbor local field. A fixed local law $\mathcal{L}$
defines the generative kernel

$$
\left(A_x(t+1),E_x(t+1)\right)
=
\mathcal{L}\left(A_x(t),E_x(t),N_{26}(x,t)\right).
$$

For a state $s_t$, define the locally reachable next-state set

$$
\mathcal{R}_x(t+1\mid s_t)
=
\left\{(A',E') : (A',E')\text{ is admissible under }\mathcal{L}\right\}.
$$

When an interaction strictly reduces that set, its information contribution can
be measured as

$$
\Delta I_x
=
\log\frac{|\mathcal{R}_x^{\mathrm{prior}}(t+1)|}
{|\mathcal{R}_x^{\mathrm{posterior}}(t+1)|}.
$$

This cardinality expression covers reduction of the reachable. Measuring a
reshaping that preserves cardinality remains an open formal problem and will
require an additional set- or distribution-sensitive measure.

The transition equation is currently a proposed model kernel, not a claimed
fundamental equation of nature. The experiments below test observable
consequences that would be expected if the organizing hypothesis is useful.

## Current evidence frontier

The foundational structural result is **Golden Claim GC-001: constraint
transport**. The latest supported bounded causal result is **GC-014: exact
precursor placement controls later articulation**. The latest prospective test
with an outcome was **GC-015**, a negative confirmation of one frozen rooted
graph-role representation. **GC-016** establishes feasibility of a marked
topology-by-state instrument; **GC-017** records that its independent
confirmation stopped before outcome measurement for insufficient support.

> In three controlled replay events across two seeds, payload-free Iota dynamics
> restored the same tracked connectivity relation through a causally necessary
> successor bridge having no cells in common with the predecessor bridge.

Finding 060 replayed all 36 previously identified Class B bridge-relocation
events. Twelve had a genuinely role-bearing predecessor bridge, and three passed
the strict transport test. In every strict positive:

- the tracked regions were connected before bridge loss;
- they were disconnected during the split;
- a disjoint successor bridge restored their reachability;
- removing that successor destroyed the restored reachability;
- pre- and post-event shortest-path length was $2$;
- payload was zero and the conservation ledger was exact.

This supports a bounded conclusion: in this event class, a relational
connectivity constraint can survive complete replacement of its local
realization. It does **not** yet establish general multi-region invariance,
indefinite persistence, uniqueness to one law, or a correspondence with physical
particles or the early universe.

Finding 061 follows the same predecessor addresses beyond rebridge. The three
strict transports retain the restored relation for `9`, `3`, and `51` recorded
offsets; all nine role-bearing Finding 060 failures have lifetime `0` at their
nominal rebridge. Each positive terminates when its one-address predecessor
lineage becomes inactive, and none undergoes a second continuous strict
transport through offset `1024`. This is evidence for finite post-transport
address persistence, not indefinite or iterated persistence.

Finding 062 replaces address identity with a preregistered prospective
expression lineage: a newly activated target inherits identity only from the
current lineage source whose successful transfer activates it. Two seed-46
strict transports continue beyond address death, extending relation lifetime
from `9` to `170` offsets and from `3` to at least `601` offsets. The latter is
still alive at the offset-`1024` censoring horizon. The seed-56 strict transport
does not propagate, and all nine role-bearing failures remain negative. No
lineage collisions or second strict transports were observed.

Finding 063 branches each strict-rebridge state across 32 new continuation RNG
streams. Handoff occurs in `16/32`, `29/32`, and `28/32` branches. The first
seed-46 state has lower susceptibility than the second seed-46 state
(`p=0.00075`) and the seed-56 state (`p=0.00251`, two-sided Fisher exact); the
two high-susceptibility states are indistinguishable in this ensemble (`p=1`).
Thus the natural seed-56 termination is a stochastic branch outcome, not proof
of an incapable state.

Finding 064 freezes a one-feature rule on 48 training branches and evaluates it
once on 48 held-out branches. The selected marker is singleton-lineage size
greater than `1.5` immediately before address death. It has held-out specificity
`1.000` but sensitivity only `0.361` (balanced accuracy `0.681`, Fisher
`p=0.021`). A preexisting descendant is therefore a sufficient marker here, but
the rule misses most handoffs created on the death tick and is rejected as a
general continuation law.

Finding 065 removes the preexisting-descendant marker and freezes a new rule on
single-address discovery rows: predict death-tick child creation when the source
has at most one active cube-26 neighbor. On 71 eligible endpoints from 96 fresh
branches, the rule fails confirmation (balanced accuracy `0.582`, Fisher
`p=0.240`). Static terminal geometry, as currently measured, is therefore
insufficient for a general continuation rule.

Finding 066 records the complete ordered hard-transfer candidate ledger on 96
fresh branches. All terminal outcomes are accounted for: 57 children created on
the death tick, 16 preexisting-descendant continuations, 19 source-exhaustion
deaths, 3 transfer-gate deaths, and 1 target-preemption death. A frozen replay
then permits exactly one naturally failed successor gate, still consuming the
original RNG draw; the branch flips from death to one collision-free inherited
child. In this bounded case, continuation is causally path-dependent: the
successor must be realized before the source spends its terminating amplitude.

Finding 067 prospectively replicates that intervention. Across 192 fresh
branches from the original source states, permitting one failed successor gate
creates a child in `6/6`; vetoing one terminal non-child spend reopens the route
in `30/30`, followed by `28/30` eventual handoffs. A complete 128-case screen of
16 unused initialization seeds then freezes two new geometries before terminal
outcomes. Across 64 branches from them, the same policy creates a child in `8/8`
successor-gate deaths, reopens one exhaustion route without eventual rescue, and
reopens one preempted target followed by handoff. Opportunity is therefore
causal but not sufficient: a lawful successor must also be realized while the
route remains open.

Finding 068 reverses the intervention and tests realized-child necessity. A
factual-only stage freezes 164 natural unique-child events across all five
source states before any veto. Suppressing that committed child produces an
alternate child on the same tick in `5`, later in `117`, and never in `42`.
All `122` alternate-child branches hand off; all `42` without an alternative
lose the lineage at address death. The first alternative uses a different
target in `105/122`. Thus a particular realization is frequently redundant,
but continuation still requires some timely ancestry-preserving realization.

Finding 069 follows those same 164 factual continuations through a staged chain
of prospectively frozen vetoes. At each depth, the first still-realized inherited
child is committed before it is suppressed at the next depth; all branches keep
the original absolute 128-offset follow-up. Observed sequential cut depths
`1..8` contain `42, 20, 22, 17, 11, 13, 13, 5` branches. The chains contain
648 distinct branch-selector identities; replaying each complete prefix produces
2,142 gate-override executions. Nineteen branches still hand off beyond depth 8
and 2 retain the original address through the horizon. Eighty branches reuse at
least one previously selected target. Every selector chain matches, payload
remains zero, conservation is exact, and no collision occurs. This is a
prefix-minimal cut depth under the declared retained-amplitude, ordered
policy—not a graph-theoretic minimum or a count of simultaneously available
routes.

Finding 070 repeats that frozen policy on four newly screened initialization
geometries selected before continuation outcomes. Their 79 committed factual
unique-child branches produced cut counts `23, 15, 7, 9, 4, 3, 5, 2` at depths
`1..8`; 10 still handed off beyond depth 8 and one was source-survival censored
at depth 7. Every geometry produced at least one alternate continuation after
the first veto, satisfying the preregistered replication gate. The chain
contains 286 committed selector events and 923 override executions, with exact
shams, selector joins, payload, conservation, and collision checks. This
replicates ordered continuation redundancy across independent geometries under
the declared policy; it does not yet establish a policy-invariant topology.

Finding 071 preregistered the next locality-and-transportability test, but its
fresh 1,024-case screen selected 9 discovery geometries and only 3 confirmation
geometries against a frozen minimum of 6 per half. The experiment therefore
stopped before any affordance response, veto, or model was run. This is an
inconclusive cohort-support result—not evidence for or against a local field.

Finding 072 acquired 14 additional geometries and completed the sealed test on
9 discovery and 17 confirmation sources. The radius-3 local model narrowly beat
the intrinsic-only control and improved four of six response dimensions, but
its standardized MSE (`1.2887`) was worse than the discovery-mean null
(`1.2720`) and its frozen permutation test was non-significant (`p=0.3537`).
The declared local-field representation therefore failed confirmation, and the
conditional gradient experiment was not opened.

Finding 074 returned to causal successor structure. A factual-only audit of 37
strict transports identified 35 eligible pre-articulation channels and froze
one in each of 25 independent initialization seeds before intervention. Erasing
the precursor activation or moving its exact post-transfer state to a frozen
nearby depleted address suppressed the recorded later split in `25/25`.
Preserving activation and amplitude at the exact address while erasing inherited
`R/V/P/angular_L` preserved the split in `18/25`, exceeding the preregistered
`17/25` composite threshold; the other seven channels require additional state
from that bundle. All 150 paired replays were exact, payload-free, and
ledger-exact. Rebridge veto extended split lifetime in `23/25`, but no channel
remained separated for 128 offsets, so durable morphogenesis was not supported.

Finding 075 asked whether a declared rooted graph role predicts the distribution
of reachable articulation futures. A new 1,536-case screen yielded 46 strict
transports in 29 seeds; factual precursor and common-donor gates froze 25
independent topology channels before outcomes. Across 16 common RNG branches per
channel, natural, role-preserving, and role-breaking states produced `20/400`,
`24/400`, and `14/400` articulations. Discordances favored preservation `21:11`,
but the frozen exact test gave `p=0.0551`, and only `5/25` preserving rates were
strictly closer to natural versus a required `17/25`. The composite graph-role
gate failed. All controls and 1,200 branch outcomes were exact. Findings 076 and
077 were therefore not opened.

Finding 078 separated structural placement from inherited state marks and
enumerated a pre-gate marked successor graph. All 175 development states were
exact; `25/25` channels were factorially identifiable and `23/25` showed a
nonzero instrument interaction. This supports feasibility only, not prediction.

Finding 079 then acquired 1,536 completely new factual continuations. Thirty
strict transports occurred in 22 independent seeds, and precursor screening
retained exactly 20 independent channels. The first common-donor audit failure
(seed 554) irreversibly reduced the maximum cohort below the frozen minimum of
20. Four independent seeds ultimately lacked a valid pair, leaving 16 before
the final repair-interaction filter. No branches or outcomes were opened. The
state-conditioned repair-affordance hypothesis therefore remains untested.

## Rig overview

The implementation began as a minimal octal-first iota universe test rig.

Default iota state:

```text
Iota = (A, R, V, P)
A = amplitude, octal 0..7
R = range, octal 0..7
V = vector, 0..63, representable as octal 00..77
P = payload, octal 0..7
```

The grid defaults to `64 x 64 x 64` and can be raised toward `256 x 256 x 256` if memory and runtime allow.

The default baseline is stamped in `config.yaml` as:

```yaml
simulation_version: iota-rig-v0.1.1
experiment:
  objective: Single active iota baseline
```

For the default `64^3` grid with one active iota, the initial density is:

```text
1 / 262144 = 0.000381%
```

The config keeps `init.total_iotas` and `init.initial_density` as `auto`; the runner resolves and logs them for each experiment.

Vector state `V` remains a 64-state octal field. With the default `cube26` mode, those states are interpreted as:

```text
0-25   move
26-51  reflect
52-63  diffuse
```

### Durable long-running commands

Run experiments expected to outlive an interactive session through the generic
heartbeat wrapper:

```bash
python3 scripts/run_with_heartbeat.py \
  --name finding074-screen \
  --heartbeat-seconds 30 \
  -- .venv/bin/python src/example_experiment.py --workers 4
```

The wrapper streams normal output while durably writing an untracked timestamped
log and an atomically replaced state record under `.run_state/`. Inspect the
latest state without depending on chat history:

```bash
cat .run_state/latest-finding074-screen.json
tail -f "$(python3 -c 'import json; print(json.load(open(".run_state/latest-finding074-screen.json"))["log_path"])')"
```

The state records command text, Git commit, PID, timestamps, output counts, and
the most recent output lines. Do not place credentials or secrets directly in a
wrapped command because the command is intentionally recorded locally.

If `--objective` is omitted, the runner uses `experiment.objective` from `config.yaml`.

The SQLite database lives at:

```text
data/iota_lab.db
```

Each run creates a numbered experiment:

```text

```

`metrics.csv`, the database, and the dashboard include `coherence_ratio`, defined as:

## Artifact retention

PNG files stay on disk; the database stores artifact metadata, paths, hashes, byte counts, and retention state. This keeps experiment queries fast while still making generated evidence auditable.

Default retention keeps the initial snapshot, final snapshot, every fourth intermediate snapshot, comparison plots, and CSV metrics:

```yaml
artifacts:

  delete_unregistered: false
```

For large sweeps, lower `run.snapshot_every` only when you need dense visual evidence. Metrics are recorded every tick regardless of PNG retention.

## Experiment safety

Every new run compares its flattened user-configured inputs against the previous experiment in `data/iota_lab.db`.

If one parameter changed, the run records:

```text
Changed parameter: init.active_iotas 64 -> 128
```

After the baseline, the runner refuses to start unless exactly one controlled parameter changed. This prevents duplicate runs and multi-parameter changes from entering the lab history as normal experiments.

The protocol is documented in `docs/experimental_protocol.md`.

Every experiment also records `simulation_version`, so results from changed simulation rules can be separated from controlled parameter sweeps.

Version changes are lineage boundaries: use a new baseline or an explicit equivalence gate before comparing across `simulation_version` values. Future branching experiments should use an explicit parent experiment rather than relying only on chronological order.

## Valence

`constants.valence` controls the maximum number of simultaneous productive relationships an active iota can maintain after local candidate scoring. It is not direct copying: candidates are scored by the existing influence rule, then conservation, decay, payload transfer, and rounding still decide what persists.

```yaml
constants:
  valence: 1
  bond_probability: 0.0
```

The original v0.1.0 behavior is equivalent to `valence: 1`.

`constants.bond_probability` gates non-primary productive relationship slots after candidate scoring. The primary vector target remains deterministic; each additional valence slot independently admits the next highest-scored candidate, then the existing conservation, payload, vector-inheritance, and rounding rules apply unchanged. This is a ranked, directed, state-dependent edge-occupation rule, not ordinary independent bond percolation.

## Openness Timeline

The committor machinery from Finding 020 is now generalized into an openness instrument. At a saved state `s_t`, the runner launches independent future random continuations and estimates:

```text
O(t) = H(Pr(outcome class | s_t)) / log(3)
```

The three outcome classes are `absorber`, `high_dominant`, and `protected`, classified under an explicit component graph such as `cube26`. Low `O(t)` means the checkpoint has a nearly closed future under the sampled horizon; high `O(t)` means the future remains non-degenerate.

Development-scale run:

```bash
python src/openness_timeline.py \
  --config config.yaml \
  --seed 42 \
  --ticks 1024 \
  --checkpoint-every 256 \
  --horizon 512 \
  --samples 8 \
  --connectivity cube26
```

Paper-grade runs should increase `--samples`, `--horizon`, and lattice size only after the 64^3 protocol is stable. Outputs are written under `output/findings/`:

```text
finding_022_openness_timeline_raw.csv
finding_022_openness_timeline_summary.csv
finding_022_openness_timeline_summary.json
finding_022_openness_timeline_states/
```

Run seed `42` and seed `56` first. The runner is O(t)-first by default. Add `--with-persistence` only after the openness timeline itself is behaving; that second pass records a top-k component persistence proxy and `complexity_score = component_persistence * openness`. This is a measurement instrument, not a new law; it works against the current `iota-rig-v0.1.1` dynamics.

## Versioned v0.2 Direction

The current rig is an irreversible growth substrate. It can show grammar, nucleation, and path-dependent basin selection, but it cannot yet make mass/energy language falsifiable because it has no hard conserved ledger and no deactivation.

The proposed next law family is documented in:

```text
docs/IOTA_RIG_V0_2_LAW_SPEC.md
```

Hard conservation and exhaustion deactivation are implemented as an opt-in v0.2 law path. Do not silently add deactivation or hard conservation to existing v0.1 runs. They are version boundaries and should use a new `simulation_version`, a fresh baseline, and golden checks against the Finding 018 replay suite.

## First big question

Could the initial non-dark iota count be one?

Yes, as a testable hypothesis. A single active iota is the cleanest possible seed. The rig should ask whether one seed can create persistent, propagating, structured non-dark activity under simple exchange rules.

If one seed dies, freezes, or creates only trivial symmetry, increase to 8, then 64. If structure appears only after many active seeds, that tells us the model requires initial diversity rather than a single primordial informational asymmetry.
