## Reading (fixed in advance in the contract)
The record acts as a **medium, not a metric**. Coherences that move identically in vacuum (all at 1/3) are slowed by
different factors inside the same record, their clocks change by different factors, and some are not affected at all.
The effect is chromatic. So records of this kind cannot by themselves produce an achromatic, redshift-like effect in
this substrate, which is relevant to the redshift question. Most travellers still do not stay themselves: they become
fronts or fail to enter.

## Lay version
We sent ten kinds of traveller, all moving at the same speed, through a real footprint. Inside it they slowed down by
different amounts, their internal rhythms changed by different amounts, and a few were not affected at all. That is how
glass treats colours, not how gravity treats clocks. Gravity changes every clock by the same amount. So in these worlds,
footprints behave like a material, not like curved space-time. A material can't produce the cosmic redshift we see,
because that redshift is identical for every colour.
