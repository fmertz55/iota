## Lay version
We repeated the shadow test with four times as many collision types. The result held without exception:
in every case that left a mark, the newcomer's passage revealed everything the mark alone held. We
also asked a stricter question: does the newcomer *itself* leave carrying that record, rather than just
disturbing the background? In 60% of cases yes, and in 40% it carries the whole record out. The
shadow's information is not only *there*; a passing coherence can pick it up and take it away.
