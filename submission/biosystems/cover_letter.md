Dear Editor,

Please consider the enclosed full-length article, “Constraint Transport and
History-Dependent Successor Accessibility in IOTA: An Information-First Finite
Local Physics Model,” for publication in BioSystems.

The manuscript presents an artificial-life application of IOTA, an
information-first finite local physics research model instantiated here as a
finite stochastic lattice simulator. It asks how organization and successor
accessibility can persist through local material turnover. Its contribution is
computational and operational: registered event records, single-decision
interventions, veto branches, and an independent cohort separate the
availability of lawful continuations from their realized history. The paper
does not claim that IOTA is itself living or that the simulation establishes
physical correspondence. It also proposes a signed support-deformation
quantity and an outcome-identity ledger for prospective adjacent-possible tests
that distinguish pruning from enabling.

The closest published comparison is Fariña and Vallejo’s 2026 biological
cosmological constant, $\Lambda_B$, which uses the squared fractional expansion
rate of Fisher-information volume. This article instead reports local carrier
interventions and proposes a signed deformation plus retained/enabled/lost
identities for prospective reachable-support tests. The manuscript treats
$\Lambda_B$ as direct prior art for geometric expansion and phase-transition
framing; the two constructions neither reproduce nor independently confirm one
another.

The topic fits BioSystems’ interest in theoretical and computational accounts
of biological organization, self-organization, artificial life, and the
conditions under which organized continuities survive component replacement.
The complete simulator, frozen configurations, intervention code, evidence
manifests, and manuscript sources are available in the associated public
repository. The submission package includes a deterministic source archive,
artifact checksums, and a machine-readable build manifest.

This work used no human participants or animals and received no specific
external funding. The manuscript states the author’s competing-interest and
AI-assistance declarations. A dedicated separate technical manuscript reports
a bounded registered Dirac-QCA cross-domain result discussed here. That result
is cited only as a separate methodological application of the freeze–test–audit
workflow: it does not support the biological claims, validate the adjacent
frontier, estimate the signed deformation, or establish independent physical
architectures. A related manuscript separately develops the broader ICP
framework; neither companion manuscript is evidentiary support for this paper.

Thank you for your consideration.
